## Global visual direction

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape, cinematic film still.
- Photorealistic, grounded contemporary rural West African village; natural skin texture and believable environments.
- Premium documentary-drama cinematography, expressive but realistic acting, atmospheric practical lighting, rich restrained earth tones, detailed production design.
- No text, captions, logos, watermarks, borders, collage layouts, or split screens inside images.
- Keep all characters visually consistent across all scenes.

## Character continuity bible

- **Amara:** West African woman, about 30, medium-dark brown skin, oval face, expressive dark eyes, neat braided-back black hair, modest patterned Ankara blouse and long wrapper in warm earth tones. Her expressions move from longing and temptation to fear, remorse, and quiet determination.
- **Chike:** West African man, about 35, deep brown skin, lean hardworking build, short neat black hair, close-trimmed beard, calm thoughtful eyes, simple work shirt in muted indigo/olive tones. Practical, humble workshop owner.
- **Bako:** West African man, about 45, dark brown skin, broad build, neatly groomed beard, confident calculating expression, refined but understated traditional outfit in dark burgundy/navy with a quality cap.
- Maintain the same facial identity, age, hairstyle, skin tone, body shape, and wardrobe logic in every appearance.
